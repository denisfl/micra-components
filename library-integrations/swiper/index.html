Swiper is MIT — loaded from a CDN. The wrapper is the Micra glue.

A carousel, reactively bound

Swiper owns the carousel — touch, momentum, arrows, keyboard. Micra mirrors its active index into state, so the "Slide X of N" readout and the custom dot strip below both react. The dots also drive Swiper back (click one to jump) — the binding runs both ways.
