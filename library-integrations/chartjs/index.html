Chart.js, reactively bound and theme-aware

The escape hatch beyond Micra's native SVG Charts: a real time x-axis (Date values), drag-to-zoom + wheel/pan, and a scatter overlay. Chart.js has no theme awareness — the wrapper reads your --micra-* tokens and re-themes the canvas live on the toggle above.

·

Drag across the chart to zoom, scroll to zoom, drag to pan when zoomed. Native Micra Charts cover stacked, legend-toggle, crosshair tooltips and dual-axis — this is for the time axis, zoom/pan and scatter they don't do.
