GLightbox is MIT — loaded from a CDN. The wrapper is the Micra glue.

A lightbox gallery, reactively bound

GLightbox owns the overlay and navigation; Micra owns the data. The grid renders from state, and the lightbox mirrors its open/closed and current-slide state back — so the readout below ("Viewing image X of N") reacts as you click through. The demo images are inline SVG, so nothing loads from the network.
